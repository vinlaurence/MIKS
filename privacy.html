Miks Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what Miks handles when you use the music player, create an account, or choose an AI music feature.

Information Miks handles

  • Account information: your email address and Firebase account identifier. If you use Google sign-in, Firebase receives the authentication information needed to verify your account.
  • Music activity: favorites, saved playlists, track metadata, listening history, listening interactions, and mood labels you choose. Signed-in data is stored in Firebase/Firestore under your account.
  • Device storage: app preferences, lyrics cache, and guest listening history may be stored in your browser or app storage.
  • AI feature inputs: when you request a mood mix, playlist enhancement, or weekly reflection, the related text, mood labels, and track titles and artists are sent through Miks’ Firebase service to OpenAI to generate the requested result. Miks does not send audio recordings to these AI features.
  • Abuse prevention: catalog-search requests are briefly counted by network address in the running service to limit automated overuse. The rate-limit counter is held in memory and is not written to your account or stored as a long-term IP history.

Services Miks uses

Miks uses Firebase for account authentication and cloud storage, Google for Google sign-in, OpenAI for optional AI music features, and third-party music services such as Jamendo, MusicBrainz, and LRCLIB for playback, catalog information, or lyrics. When you use these features, the relevant service may receive connection or request information under its own privacy policy.

How information is used

Miks uses this information to sign you in, save and sync your library, show your listening profile, provide music search and lyrics, and return AI-generated music suggestions you request. Listening and mood information is used to build the personal profile and reflections shown in Miks.

Your choices

You can use Miks as a guest without creating an account. You can remove favorites, playlists, and listening history using the controls in the app. Clearing browser or app storage removes locally cached preferences and guest data. Signing out does not delete cloud data. Account deletion is not currently available in the app; contact the Miks publisher through the channel where you obtained the app to request help with account data.

Children and sensitive information

Miks is a general music app, not a health service. Mood selections are used for music discovery and are not intended to assess health. Please do not submit highly sensitive personal information in mood descriptions or other AI prompts.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated as Miks changes. The date above shows when it was last revised. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Miks publisher through the channel where you obtained the app.
